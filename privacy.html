This Privacy Policy sets out how ZeeNova Apps ("we", "us" or "our") processes, protects and discloses information in connection with EsportsCalc: Standings Maker for Android and iOS (the "App"), the team registration form available at esportscalc.web.app (the "Registration Form") and this website (collectively, the "Service").

By using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described in this Policy. Should you have any questions, we would be pleased to assist (see Section 13).

1. Scope and Application

  • This Policy governs the processing of information through the Service.
  • Third-party services, including Google Play, the Apple App Store, Unity Ads, RevenueCat and Google Firebase, are governed by their respective privacy policies, as are external websites accessed through links within the Service.
  • Content you elect to share beyond the App, such as a poster published on social media, is subject to the policies of the platform on which it is shared.

2. Information We Process

2.1 Information stored on your device

The following information is generated and stored locally on your device and remains under your control:

  • Tournaments, including names, games, matches, maps, scoring rules and results.
  • Teams and players, including team names, tags, in-game names (IGNs), IDs and roles.
  • Images you add, such as team logos, player photographs, your organizer logo, poster backgrounds, custom poster designs and map images.
  • Results extracted from your screenshots, such as names, kills and placements, together with the posters and certificates you create.
  • Team registrations, once retrieved by the App (Section 2.5).
  • App settings, such as your theme, organizer name and advertising preference.

You may delete this information within the App at any time, and uninstalling the App removes it in its entirety. Posters saved to your gallery are retained there until you remove them.

Screenshot analysis is performed locally by AI models integrated into the App, ensuring that your screenshots remain on your device.

2.2 Advertising (free plan)

The free plan is supported by advertising delivered through Unity Ads, a service of Unity Technologies ("Unity"). To serve and measure advertisements, Unity receives:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (on iOS and iPadOS, only with your permission) and other device identifiers.
  • Your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, such as your country.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version.
  • Advertising interactions, such as which advertisements were displayed, viewed, skipped or selected.
  • Diagnostic information relating to how advertisements load and play.

Unity processes this information to serve and measure advertisements, to personalize them where you have consented and to detect and prevent fraud, and may engage advertisers and measurement partners in accordance with its own policies. Paid plans are entirely ad-free, and the advertising service operates exclusively on the free plan. Your advertising preferences are described in Section 6.

2.3 Subscriptions and purchases

  • Payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store, which manage your payment details directly.
  • RevenueCat verifies purchases on our behalf and receives a randomly generated app user ID, purchase and subscription details, basic device information and your IP address.

2.4 Online team registration (organizers)

Online registration is an optional Ultra Pro feature, enabled by the organizer on a per-tournament basis. Once enabled:

  • Anonymous authentication. The App authenticates with Google Firebase using an anonymous, randomly generated identifier.
  • Event details. The information displayed on the registration form is stored on our servers, comprising the tournament name, game, start time, number of slots, entry fee, prize pool, payment method, payment number and account holder's name, the organizer's name and, where provided, the organizer's WhatsApp number.
  • Public availability. Event details are accessible to anyone holding the form's link, enabling teams to review them before registering.
  • Notifications. The App may notify the organizer when a team registers. Notifications can be managed in the device settings.

2.5 Team registrations

Teams may register through the Registration Form without installing the App or creating an account. We receive the following information and deliver it to the relevant tournament organizer:

  • Required information: the team name, a WhatsApp number and a payment screenshot.
  • Information requested at the organizer's discretion: the registrant's name, the team tag, the team logo, and the players' in-game names and IDs.

The organizer determines which information to request and uses it to administer the tournament, including confirming payments, allocating slots and sharing the WhatsApp group link. The Registration Form operates without cookies, advertising or analytics.

2.6 Communications

When you contact us by email, we receive your message and contact details and use them to respond to your enquiry.

2.7 Websites

  • This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages and operates without cookies or analytics. GitHub may retain standard server logs for security purposes.
  • The Registration Form is hosted on Google Firebase Hosting, which processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver the page securely.

2.8 Device permissions

The App requests only the permissions required for its features and collects only the information described in this Policy:

  • Photos: the App uses the system photo picker and accesses only the images you select.
  • Storage (Android 10 and earlier): used to save posters to your gallery.
  • Notifications: used for registration alerts.
  • Tracking (iOS and iPadOS): Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt lets you decide whether advertising may use your advertising identifier.

3. Purposes of Processing

  • Service provision: delivering the App's features, including screenshot scanning, standings, posters and online registration.
  • Registration delivery: transmitting each team's registration to the relevant organizer.
  • Advertising: serving and measuring advertisements on the free plan.
  • Purchases: verifying, restoring and renewing paid plans.
  • Security and integrity: protecting the Service against abuse and fraud.
  • Support: responding to your enquiries.
  • Legal compliance: meeting our legal and regulatory obligations.

We process information exclusively for these purposes, and we never sell personal information.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where data protection laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation apply, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: to provide the App, online registration and the paid plans you purchase.
  • Consent: for personalized advertising, tracking on iOS and iPadOS, and notifications. You may withdraw your consent at any time (see Section 6).
  • Legitimate interests: to secure the Service, prevent fraud and deliver the non-personalized advertising that sustains the free plan.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable law, including tax and accounting requirements.

5. Service Providers and Disclosures

Information is disclosed only to the extent necessary to operate the Service:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Ads): advertising on the free plan (Section 2.2). See the Unity Privacy Policy and the Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: purchase verification (Section 2.3). See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Google LLC (Firebase): storage and delivery of online registrations and hosting of the Registration Form, on our behalf (Sections 2.4 and 2.5). See the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store: distribution of the App and processing of payments.
  • Tournament organizers: the registration details of teams that register for their tournament.
  • Holders of a registration link: the event details the organizer elects to display.
  • Legal requirements: where required by law, or to protect the rights, property and safety of our users and others.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition or similar transaction, with notice to users.

Each provider is required to protect information to a standard equivalent to this Policy, consistent with Google Play and App Store requirements. Information is used for marketing purposes solely through the advertising described in Section 2.2.

6. Advertising Preferences

  • Within the App: select Profile → Personalized ads → Not personalized. The volume of advertising remains unchanged.
  • iOS and iPadOS: select "Ask App Not to Track" when prompted, or adjust your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: open Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices, Settings → Google → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising identifier.
  • Within an advertisement: Unity's Data Privacy icon provides access to Unity's privacy controls.
  • Ad-free experience: available with any paid plan.

7. Data Retention

  • On-device data: retained until deleted within the App or until the App is uninstalled.
  • Team registrations: removed from our servers once retrieved by the organizer's App, typically within minutes.
  • Event details: removed from our servers one day after the tournament's start time, upon the close of registration for tournaments without a start time, or upon deletion of the tournament.
  • Residual data: should an organizer's App cease to connect, for example following uninstallation, we will remove the associated data upon request accompanied by the form's link.
  • Anonymous identifier: retained so that the App can continue to manage its forms. It identifies the App installation only.
  • Advertising data: retained by Unity in accordance with its policies.
  • Purchase data: retained by RevenueCat for as long as necessary to provide your purchases and to satisfy tax and accounting requirements.
  • Correspondence: retained for as long as necessary to resolve your enquiry.

8. Data Security

We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information, including:

  • Encrypted (HTTPS) connections between the App and every service it uses.
  • Server-side access controls that restrict each organizer's registrations to that organizer's App.
  • Your device's own safeguards, such as its screen lock, which protect on-device data.

Although no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, we will notify affected users and the competent authorities of any security incident as required by law.

9. Your Rights

  • App data: delete tournaments, teams and players at any time, or uninstall the App to remove all on-device data.
  • Advertising: manage your preferences as described in Section 6.
  • Notifications: manage them in your device settings.
  • Team registrations: the organizer can delete a registration within the App, or you may contact us directly.
  • Purchase data: send us your Google Play or Apple order number, and we will request that RevenueCat delete the related records.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel them in Google Play (Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) or, on iOS and iPadOS, under Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Subscriptions remain active until cancelled, including after the App is uninstalled.

Depending on your place of residence, including under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation and US state privacy laws, you may have the right to access, rectify, erase or port your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Where sharing an advertising identifier for personalized advertising constitutes a "sale" or "sharing" under US state law, selecting Not personalized in the App, or "Ask App Not to Track" on iOS, exercises your right to opt out. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us; we respond within 30 days.

10. Children's Data

The Service, including the Registration Form, is intended for tournament organizers and players aged 13 and over. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Unity, RevenueCat, Google and the app store operators may process information in the United States and other jurisdictions whose data protection laws may differ from those of your country. Where required by law, we rely on the appropriate safeguards maintained by these providers.
